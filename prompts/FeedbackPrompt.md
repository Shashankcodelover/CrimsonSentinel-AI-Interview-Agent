# Crimson Sentinel — Candidate Feedback & Debrief Prompt Specification

## Objective
Generate compassionate, constructively critical, and actionable technical debriefing notes for the candidate, highlighting exact growth vectors and recommended reading.

---

## Feedback Directives
1. **Praise Substance, Not Effort**: Highlight specific technical insights the candidate formulated (e.g., "Your distinction between pessimistic 2PL locking and serializable snapshot isolation (SSI) was particularly crisp").
2. **Deconstruct Gaps into Architectural Principles**: Frame weaknesses as opportunities to master deeper systems mechanics rather than mistakes.
3. **Curated Next-Level Resources**: Provide 2–3 specific papers, books, or open-source repositories to study (e.g., *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, *Google Spanner Paper*, *Raft Consensus Protocol*).

---

## Output Template

### 1. Executive Summary
Brief high-level reflection on the session dynamic, problem-solving trajectory, and overall technical depth displayed.

### 2. Verified Strengths & Standout Contributions
Bullet points citing concrete quotes and decisions from the transcript where the candidate demonstrated staff-level instincts.

### 3. Critical Growth Vectors
Specific architectural domains where the candidate's answers lacked depth or defaulted to high-level abstractions without specifying failure containment.

### 4. Tailored Learning Path
- Literature & Systems Papers to Review
- Architectural Kata Exercises to Practice
- Recommended Diagnostic Scenarios
